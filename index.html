Our approach

Moving is personal. Immigration is technical. We help with both.

A country can be generous in spirit and demanding on paper.

Life in Argentina is a boutique legal and relocation firm for people who want to live, work, invest or build something here. We combine legal work with practical local support, so you can understand what applies to your situation and what comes next.

What we do

One local team for the parts of Argentina that are hardest to navigate from the outside.

Residency and citizenship

Our city

Buenos Aires is not just where we work. It is where our lives are.

We live in Palermo Soho. We know the streets, cafés, institutions and everyday logistics that make Buenos Aires wonderful and, sometimes, complicated. Life in Argentina grew from wanting to help other people make this city feel like their own.
