About us

This is our city.

We live in Palermo Soho, and we love Buenos Aires in a way that is difficult to explain without getting a little sentimental about it.

This is our city. We know its streets, its cafés, its trees, the old buildings squeezed between new ones, the quiet of a Sunday morning and the complete chaos of a weekday afternoon.

Like everyone who lives here, we have our complaints. Buenos Aires can be complicated, unpredictable and, at times, exhausting. But that is also part of what makes it what it is. For all its contradictions, we genuinely believe there are few places quite like it. And for the right person, living here can be something very special.

Why we do this

Why Buenos Aires matters to us.

There is something Borges understood very well about Buenos Aires. He loved it not because it was grand or monumental, but because it was intimate.

A collection of streets, corners and neighborhoods tied to memory, time and a sense of belonging. We feel something very similar.

Buenos Aires is not just where we happen to live or where our office is. It is part of our lives, our memories and who we are.

Life in Argentina grew out of that feeling. We wanted to help people who choose Argentina understand the place we know and love, and make a life here that feels like their own.

Yes, there is paperwork, immigration law and bureaucracy involved. That is our job. But behind all of that is something much more personal: we really want people to discover why we chose to make our lives here too.
